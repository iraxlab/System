Rapport & Interpersonal Alignment

NLP · Rapport & Interpersonal Alignment. Building trust and responsiveness, ethically.

  1. Physiological Matching & Mirroring · Gently copying a person's posture, speed and tone so they feel at ease with you.
  2. Cross-Over Mirroring · Matching one of a person's rhythms with a different part of your body.
  3. Vocal Pacing · Matching how a person speaks: tone, speed, volume and pauses.
  4. Verbal Pacing & Backtracking · Repeating what someone said, using their own important words.
  5. Pacing & Leading · First meeting a person where they are, then guiding them somewhere new.
  6. Breaking Rapport · Breaking the connection on purpose, to stop a mood or end a talk politely.
