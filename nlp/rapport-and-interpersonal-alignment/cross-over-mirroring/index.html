Cross-Over Mirroring

What it is. Matching one of a person's rhythms with a different part of your body.

What it gives you. You build rapport without copying them openly.

Steps

  1. Notice one rhythm in the person: breathing, blinking or nodding.
  2. Choose a different, small movement of your own: a finger, a foot or your voice.
  3. Match their rhythm with it. Example: tap a finger each time they breathe out.
  4. Keep it small and natural.
  5. Notice if they feel more at ease.

Time. A few minutes

Words to know

Cross-over
Matching one behaviour with a different one, such as breathing with a hand movement.

Source. Bandler & Grinder, 1979
