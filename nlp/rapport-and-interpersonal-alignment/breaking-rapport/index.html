Breaking Rapport

What it is. Breaking the connection on purpose, to stop a mood or end a talk politely.

What it gives you. You can end a conversation kindly, without being rude.

Steps

  1. Decide the talk should end or the mood should change.
  2. Turn your body slightly away.
  3. Change your speed or volume.
  4. Look away for a moment.
  5. End with a warm, clear sentence.

Time. A few seconds

Words to know

Mismatch
Doing the opposite of the other person, on purpose.
