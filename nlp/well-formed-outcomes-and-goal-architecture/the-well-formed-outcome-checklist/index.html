The Well-Formed Outcome Checklist

What it is. A checklist that turns a wish into a clear goal.

What it gives you. A clear goal is much easier to reach than a vague wish.

Steps

  1. Say what you want, not what you don't want: 'I want calm', not 'I don't want stress'.
  2. Picture it done: what do you see, hear and feel?
  3. Decide how you will know you have it.
  4. Check that it is up to you, not someone else.
  5. Decide where, when and with whom.
  6. List what you already have and what you still need.
  7. Ask: what will I gain, and what will I give up?
  8. Take the first small step today.

Time. 15 minutes

Words to know

Outcome
The result you want.
Evidence
The sign that shows you got it. For example: 'I sleep eight hours a night.'
