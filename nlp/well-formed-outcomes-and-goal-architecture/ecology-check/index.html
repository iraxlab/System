Ecology Check

What it is. Checking that a change is good for your whole life, not just one part of it.

What it gives you. A change that hurts another part of your life does not last.

Steps

  1. Name the change you want.
  2. Ask: what will I gain if I get it?
  3. Ask: what will I lose if I get it?
  4. Ask: what happens if I don't get it?
  5. Think about family, work, health and money.
  6. Adjust the change until every answer feels right.

Time. 10 minutes

Words to know

Ecology
Here it means: is this change good for me, for the people around me, and for my life as a whole?

Source. Bandler & Grinder · Cartesian questions
