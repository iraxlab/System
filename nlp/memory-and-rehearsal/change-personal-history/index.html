Change Personal History

What it is. Taking a strong good feeling back into old memories of a bad one.

What it gives you. Old memories feel different when you bring help into them.

Safety. Mild states only. For anxiety, depression or trauma, work with a qualified clinician.

Steps

  1. First, link a good feeling to one knuckle, as in Resource Anchor.
  2. Name a small bad feeling that keeps coming back.
  3. Remember the most recent time you felt it.
  4. Press the knuckle while you remember.
  5. Go to an earlier time. Keep pressing.
  6. Notice how each memory feels different.
  7. Imagine a future moment. Notice the new feeling.

Time. 20 minutes

Words to know

Personal history
Your past memories, and how they feel to you now.

Source. Bandler & Grinder, 1979
