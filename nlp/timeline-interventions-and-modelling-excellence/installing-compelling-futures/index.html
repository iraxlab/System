Installing Compelling Futures

What it is. Placing one clear goal on a date in your imagined future.

What it gives you. A goal with a date and a clear picture feels real and close.

Steps

  1. Choose one goal with a clear date.
  2. Picture the moment it is done: what you see, hear and feel.
  3. Make the picture bright, clear and close.
  4. Imagine your life as a line: the past behind you, the future in front.
  5. In your mind, carry the picture forward to the date and place it there.
  6. See the steps between today and that date.
  7. Come back to today and take the first step.

Time. 10 minutes

Words to know

Time line
Your life imagined as a line: yesterday behind you, tomorrow in front.

Source. Tad James, time line goal setting
