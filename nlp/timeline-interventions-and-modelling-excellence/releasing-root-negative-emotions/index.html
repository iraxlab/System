Releasing Root Negative Emotions

What it is. Releasing an old negative emotion by going above your timeline to before the first event that caused it.

What it gives you. When you keep the learning from the past, the emotion can be released.

Safety. Not for trauma, PTSD or depression. Stop at any time; work with a qualified clinician.

Steps

  1. Choose one emotion. The standard order is anger, sadness, fear, hurt, guilt. Start with anger.
  2. Check that you are ready, and that it is right to let it go.
  3. Float high above your timeline.
  4. Go back to just before the first event connected to this emotion.
  5. Stay above it and before it. Never step into the event.
  6. Ask: 'What can I learn from this, so I can let the emotion go?'
  7. Keep the learning. Notice the emotion is gone from this point.
  8. Float back to now along the timeline.
  9. Check later memories. Then repeat with the next emotion in the order.

Time. 15 minutes

Words to know

Root cause
The first event connected to a feeling.
The five emotions
The standard order in Time Line Therapy: anger, sadness, fear, hurt, guilt.

Source. Tad James & Wyatt Woodsmall, Time Line Therapy, 1988
