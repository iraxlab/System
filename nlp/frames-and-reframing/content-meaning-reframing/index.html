Content (Meaning) Reframing

What it is. Finding another meaning for the same event.

What it gives you. When the meaning changes, the feeling changes.

Steps

  1. Write down the complaint in the person's own words.
  2. Ask: 'What else could this mean?'
  3. Look for the good reason behind the behaviour.
  4. Offer it softly: 'Is it possible that…?'
  5. Example: 'He's too controlling' becomes 'He wants to protect you.'
  6. Watch their face and breathing relax.
  7. Never say their feeling is wrong.

Time. A few minutes

Words to know

Reframe
Seeing the same thing in a new way. Like turning a photo to see it from another side.

Source. Bandler & Grinder, Reframing, 1982 · James Gross, cognitive reappraisal
