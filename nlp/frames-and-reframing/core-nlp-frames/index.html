Core NLP Frames

What it is. Six standard frames that guide a conversation: outcome, ecology, evidence, as if, contrast and relevancy.

What it gives you. Each frame keeps a talk focused in a different way.

Steps

  1. Learn the six frames, then use them in conversation.
  2. Outcome frame: 'What do you want?'
  3. Ecology frame: 'Is this good for you and for others?'
  4. Evidence frame: 'How will you know you have it?'
  5. As-if frame: 'Imagine it is done. What did you do?'
  6. Contrast frame: 'What is different between when it works and when it doesn't?'
  7. Relevancy frame: 'How does this connect to our goal?'
  8. Choose the frame that fits the moment.

Time. 10 minutes
