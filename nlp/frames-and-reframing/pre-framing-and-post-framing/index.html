Pre-Framing & Post-Framing

What it is. Setting the meaning before something happens, and confirming it after.

What it gives you. People know what to expect, and leave with the right meaning.

Steps

  1. Before: decide the meaning you want people to take away.
  2. Pre-frame: 'In the next ten minutes we'll do a simple exercise. Some parts may feel unusual, and that is normal.'
  3. Do the activity.
  4. Post-frame: 'Notice what changed. That is what this exercise is for.'
  5. Ask what they will take away.

Time. A few minutes

Words to know

Pre-frame
Setting expectations before an event.
Post-frame
Fixing the meaning right after an event.
