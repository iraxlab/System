Dilts' Neurological Levels (Logical Levels)

What it is. Six levels of who you are, from where you are to who you are.

What it gives you. A change at a higher level changes everything below it.

Steps

  1. Choose a hard situation. Mark six spots in a line on the floor.
  2. Spot 1, Where: the place, the time, who is there.
  3. Step back to spot 2, What: what you do there.
  4. Spot 3, How: the skills you use.
  5. Spot 4, Why: what matters to you.
  6. Spot 5, Who: who you are. Find a symbol for it.
  7. Spot 6, Purpose: what larger whole you are part of, and what you serve beyond yourself.
  8. Walk forward through every spot, taking the symbol with you.
  9. Back at the situation, notice what has changed.

Time. 20 minutes

Words to know

Neurological levels
Robert Dilts' six levels: environment, behaviour, capabilities, beliefs and values, identity, purpose.
Identity
Who you are, not only what you do.

Source. Robert Dilts, after Gregory Bateson
