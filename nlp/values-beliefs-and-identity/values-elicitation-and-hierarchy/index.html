Values Elicitation & Hierarchy

What it is. Finding what matters most to you, in order.

What it gives you. Your choices get easier when you know your order.

Steps

  1. Choose one area of life, such as work.
  2. Ask: 'What is important to me about work?'
  3. Keep asking 'What else?' until nothing comes.
  4. Choose the most important. Then the next.
  5. Number the list.
  6. Test: 'If I had everything except number one, would I be happy?'
  7. Use the list to make choices.

Time. 20 minutes

Words to know

Values
What matters most to you, such as freedom, family or health.
