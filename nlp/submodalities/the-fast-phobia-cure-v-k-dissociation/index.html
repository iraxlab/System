The Fast Phobia Cure (V-K Dissociation)

What it is. Watching a frightening memory like an old film from far away, so the fear gets smaller.

What it gives you. Distance makes the fear smaller while you look at the memory.

Safety. Not a substitute for phobia or trauma treatment. Work with a qualified clinician.

Steps

  1. Imagine sitting in a cinema.
  2. Float to the back room, where the projector is.
  3. From there, watch yourself in the seat, watching the screen.
  4. On the screen: a black and white picture of the moment before the memory.
  5. Play the film quickly to the moment after, when you were safe.
  6. Then play it backwards, very fast, in colour.
  7. Repeat 3 to 5 times.
  8. Think of it gently. Notice the difference.

Time. 15 minutes

Words to know

Phobia
A strong fear of something specific, such as spiders.
Double distance
Watching yourself watching yourself. It makes the memory feel far away.
