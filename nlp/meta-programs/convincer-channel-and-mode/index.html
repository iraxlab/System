Convincer Channel & Mode

What it is. Finding how a person becomes convinced: through which sense, and how often.

What it gives you. You give proof in the way the person actually decides.

Steps

  1. Ask: 'How do you know someone is good at their job?'
  2. Channel: do they need to see it, hear it, read it or do it with them?
  3. Ask: 'How often must they show it before you are convinced?'
  4. Mode: automatically, a number of times, over a period of time, or consistently every time.
  5. Give proof in their channel and mode.
  6. Respect a no.

Time. 10 minutes

Words to know

Convincer channel
The sense used to gather proof: see, hear, read or do.
Convincer mode
How often proof is needed: automatic, number of times, period of time, consistent.
