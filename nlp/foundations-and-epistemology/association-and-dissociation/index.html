Association & Dissociation

What it is. Seeing a memory through your own eyes, or watching yourself from outside.

What it gives you. Inside makes a feeling stronger. Outside makes it smaller.

Safety. For painful memories or trauma, work with a qualified clinician.

Steps

  1. Remember a happy moment, through your own eyes.
  2. Notice how strong the feeling is.
  3. Now see yourself in that memory, as if on a screen.
  4. Notice the feeling gets smaller.
  5. Do the same with a mildly unpleasant memory, from outside.
  6. Make that picture smaller and further away.
  7. The rule: step into good memories. Step out of bad ones.

Time. 5 minutes

Words to know

Inside
Looking out through your own eyes, as if you are there again.
Outside
Watching yourself in the memory, like watching a film.
