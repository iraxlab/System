Sensory Predicates & Translation

What it is. Listening for seeing, hearing and feeling words, and saying an idea in each of them.

What it gives you. Your message reaches more people, more easily.

Steps

  1. Seeing words: see, clear, look, picture.
  2. Hearing words: sounds, hear, rings a bell.
  3. Feeling words: feel, grasp, solid. Thinking words: makes sense, consider.
  4. Notice which kind a person uses most right now.
  5. Answer with the same kind of words.
  6. Translate: 'I see what you mean' → 'That sounds right' → 'That feels right' → 'That makes sense.'
  7. When you explain something important, say it once in each sense.

Time. Any conversation

Words to know

Predicate
A sensory word, such as 'see', 'hear' or 'feel', that shows which sense a person is using.
Auditory digital
Self-talk and logic words, such as 'makes sense'.

Source. Bandler & Grinder, representational systems
