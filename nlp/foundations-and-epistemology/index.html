Foundations & Epistemology

NLP · Foundations & Epistemology. How perception, the senses and attention work.

  1. The NLP Communication Model · NLP is a set of simple tools for changing how you think, feel and act.
  2. The Presuppositions of NLP · The basic working beliefs of NLP. You act as if they are true because they are useful.
  3. Calibration · Noticing small changes in a person's face, skin and breathing.
  4. Representational Systems (VAKOG) · The five senses the mind uses to build inner experience: seeing, hearing, feeling, smelling and tasting.
  5. Sensory Predicates & Translation · Listening for seeing, hearing and feeling words, and saying an idea in each of them.
  6. Eye Accessing Cues · An old NLP idea that the direction of the eyes shows how a person is thinking.
  7. Synesthesia & Overlap · How one sense can trigger another, like a smell bringing back a picture.
  8. Association & Dissociation · Seeing a memory through your own eyes, or watching yourself from outside.
  9. Uptime & Downtime · Two modes of attention: outward to the world (uptime) or inward to your thoughts (downtime).
