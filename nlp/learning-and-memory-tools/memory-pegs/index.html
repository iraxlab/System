Memory Pegs

What it is. Remembering by placing pictures in places you know well.

What it gives you. Your mind finds pictures in familiar places easily.

Steps

  1. Choose ten places in your home, in order.
  2. Learn the order: door, hall, kitchen…
  3. Turn each thing to remember into a funny, clear picture.
  4. Put each picture in one place.
  5. To remember, walk through your home in your mind.
  6. Go over it once more the same day.

Time. 15 minutes

Words to know

Memory palace
A place you know well, used to store things you want to remember.

Source. Method of loci, Greek and Roman orators
