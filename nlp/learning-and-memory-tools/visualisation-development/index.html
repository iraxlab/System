Visualisation Development

What it is. Exercises to see pictures in your mind more clearly.

What it gives you. Many tools here use mind pictures; practice makes them clearer.

Steps

  1. Look at a simple object for ten seconds.
  2. Close your eyes and picture it.
  3. Open your eyes and compare.
  4. Picture it again and change its colour.
  5. Change its size, then how far away it is.
  6. Practise every day with more detailed objects.

Time. 5 minutes a day

Words to know

Visualise
Make a picture in your mind.
