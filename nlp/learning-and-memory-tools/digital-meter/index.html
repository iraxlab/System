Digital Meter

What it is. Adding numbers in your head by picturing a counter that rolls on.

What it gives you. The picture keeps count for you.

Steps

  1. Picture a digital counter showing zero.
  2. Watch it roll to the first number.
  3. Add the next number. Watch the digits roll on.
  4. Let the numbers carry over by themselves.
  5. Read the final number.
  6. Practise with small numbers first.

Time. 5 minutes

Words to know

Counter
A display that counts up, like a car's mileage.
