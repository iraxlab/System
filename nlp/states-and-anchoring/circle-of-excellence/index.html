Circle of Excellence

What it is. An imagined circle on the floor that holds your best feeling.

What it gives you. You can step into confidence whenever you need it.

Safety. Positive states only. If distress appears, stop and step out.

Steps

  1. Imagine a circle on the floor. Give it a colour.
  2. Remember a time you felt very confident.
  3. Let the feeling grow.
  4. At its strongest, step into the circle.
  5. Let the feeling fill you. Then step out.
  6. Repeat 3 to 5 times.
  7. Think of a coming moment. Step in, and take the feeling with you.

Time. 10 minutes

Words to know

Resource
Anything that helps you: a feeling, a skill, a memory.

Source. John Grinder & Judith DeLozier
