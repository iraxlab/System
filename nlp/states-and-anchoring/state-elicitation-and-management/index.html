State Elicitation & Management

What it is. Bringing back a feeling on purpose by reliving a time you had it.

What it gives you. You can choose your state instead of waiting for it.

Steps

  1. Choose the state, such as confidence.
  2. Ask: 'Can you remember a specific time you felt totally confident?'
  3. Go back to that time: see what you saw, hear what you heard, feel what you felt.
  4. Look through your own eyes, as if you are there.
  5. Let the feeling grow.
  6. Notice the signs in the body: breathing, posture, face.
  7. Shake it off, and notice that you can return to it.

Time. 10 minutes

Words to know

State
The whole of how you feel right now, in mind and body.
Elicit
Bring out, on purpose.

Source. Bandler & Grinder
